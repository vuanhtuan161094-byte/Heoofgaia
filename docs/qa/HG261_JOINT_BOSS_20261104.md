# HG-26.1 — Three Commanders × Two Stacks in ONE Real Boss Battle

**Authoritative correction, owner 2026-11-04.** No rotating three separate teams. A joint Boss raid has **1–3 distinct heroes and at most 2 individually owned stacks from each**, thus at most **6 allied stacks in the same 12×9 tactical encounter**, rather than three separate successive battles. The 3×2 rule already applies in Tower HG21 and now also applies to HG26 Boss world hunts.

## Implemented sources
- `engine.js`: real world Boss stack's `heroId` controls per-owner attack/defense and equipment/combat modifiers, exactly as the HG21 Tower branch; joint fights still deduct **real** wounded/survivor army through canonical casualty accounting.
- `hg21-tower-tactical.js`: per-commander cast and cooldown isolation is available to joint Boss battles; alliance buffs target only that hero's two owned stacks; original Tower behavior unchanged.
- `hg261-joint-boss.js`: opt-in joint party snapshot (IDs, up to 2 selected real stacks each), cross-hero anti-duplicate reservation, one canonical lead expedition with a consolidated six-stack formation and true per-member owner tags, one AP payment per actual sortie, real duration to and from Boss, auto-attack via real tactical turns, Arena and real recruitment recovery, partial troop loss, paid progression, Save v07 metadata `hg26.joint` and optional `hg26.config.team`.
- `app.js`: Quân đội → HG-26 lets player select up to three heroes and choose up to two troop slots for each; separate **refresh selected hero's troop slots** action. In battle show each stack's hero name and individual per-hero skill panel. No animation, canvas painter, sprite or CSS pipeline was changed for HG26.1.
- Legacy HG26 solo saved policies without a `team` remain readable. Default farm is OFF. Game and UI keep Save version `7`; no new storage key.

## Accounting and strict requirements
1. The real city warehouse is the only troop source. Preflight checks selected stack counts against nonparticipant reservations; an identical soldier cannot join two heroes. The team may include fewer than three heroes if the player has not yet recruited three.
2. The joint expedition is **one world march**. The canonical lead `E.startMarch` charges AP once; remaining owners' soldiers are removed from stock exactly once into the same `s.expedition.army`, then displayed together as six stacks in the single Boss battle. Their distinct `heroId` values drive all battle buffs and spells. The combined army returns only once.
3. Battle is resolved by the original per-turn tactical engine. AI casualties decrement the corresponding `s.expedition.formation` slots and aggregate `s.expedition.army`, and generate actual Arena wounded queues. Do not turn this into Tower's non-loss projection.
4. Victory goes through canonical HG24/HG20 loot and battle report exactly once. Secondary participating heroes gain one real-victory XP receipt each, **not** duplicated gold, shards, rank rewards, or reports. Two consecutive joint victories produce two actual marches and two distinct battle reports.
5. Safe policies (AP reserve, troops, percentage coverage, casualty stop, paid healing, paid recruiting, total sorties) stay active; when losses exceed the owner threshold mid-battle, further automated turns stop and player retains the real battlefield.
6. While the raid is outside, all participating heroes are treated as busy even though the canonical expedition stores the lead ID. Disallow extra troop assignment, Castellan appointment, rebirth and selecting a deployed commander; old Hero Tower 3×2 remains separate. Existing Tower selections involving deployed heroes are held and restored when their army returns and legitimate formations are available.
