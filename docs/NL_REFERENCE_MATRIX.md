- **VERIFIED** means a claim is publicly stated or displayed on a cited official page as checked on the date above; it does not verify hidden formulas or historical builds.
- **DERIVED** means a design adaptation owned by this project.
- **SIMULATED** means project-authored formulas, balance, chance or content.
- **UNKNOWN** means available official sources do not establish the claim.
