## 1. North star (mandatory) / Style identity
- **Primary:** original 2D hand-painted illustration and 2D painted game sprites. Historical Three Kingdoms fantasy, clear silhouettes, rich pigments and organic brush texture. Painterly shading, believable cloth/armor/wood/stone, soft atmospheric perspective. Not flat geometric wireframes, rough CSS silhouettes, retro pixel art, 3D models masquerading as 2D, or AI-looking inconsistent cutouts.
- **Recommended pilot (awaiting owner visual approval):** *thủy mặc có màu, bán tả thực*: Chinese brush/ink contours + deep but restrained natural colors. Ink is a texture and expressive edge, not a compulsory black-and-white filter. Keep evocative army reds, jade, indigo and gold accents, with grounded neutral terrain.
- **Alternative sub-styles for owner choice** before full-scale production: (A) traditional parchment/ink-wash atmospheric; (B) richly painted semi-realistic 2D (recommended balance); (C) bright storybook hand-painted 2D. All remain original and functional. Do not freeze aesthetics of entire roster until pilot approval.
- No blanket default to chibi, pixel, manga outline or old-fashioned mobile game icon style. General proportions, anatomy and faces should suit dramatic historical painted illustration; battle troops must remain legible at game scale.
- UI: parchment/ink-panel motifs, beautiful readable modern Vietnamese typography, consistent 8px system grid/spacing, clear contrast and states, no garish gradients or excessive fake gold ornament.

## 2. Coverage and concrete specifications
1. **World map:** large original hand-painted 2D geography, mountains/rivers/fields/roads/cities; region landmarks and click targets separated as live interaction layer. Prefer modular paintable tiles/overlays for scalable world rather than one non-interactive enormous baked bitmap. Captured/hostile/ally states visibly distinct. Must support path/ETA/armies over painted terrain.
2. **City:** original detailed 2D illustration with buildings as separate layer/sprites for Palace, Barracks, Farm, Market, Mine, Lumberyard, Academy, Tavern, Walls and discoverable events. Each building has distinct view/selection/upgrade/tap target; not just a pasted painting with transparent click map lacking feedback.
3. **Generals:** hand-painted portrait bust + matching smaller tactical full-body sprite, historical-inspired armor and facial character, distinct class/palette/weapon silhouette. Portraits and field sprites must share identity but can use different composition and rendering detail.
4. **Troops:** frame-by-frame 2D hand-painted sprites, four cardinal facing directions, action baseline Idle/Walk/Attack/Hurt/Fall, alpha-transparent, consistent ground pivot and 2D registration. **NO skeletal rigs**. Use discrete pose drawings or honest disclosed 2D compositing with independent manually cleaned key poses; never describe algorithmic pose morph as independently drawn. Core battle animation must contain real movement, timing and visible hit response; test at actual mobile scale.
5. **Battlefield:** original environment layers (far background, terrain, shadows, tactical feedback), larger readable troop groups with spacing/depth. Weapons/contact align correctly, moving enemies remain targetable. HP/morale/status in readable diegetic HUD, not floating text overload.
6. **Skill VFX:** painted energy/smoke/brush-mark FX with transparent alpha, localized glow, charge → cast → travel → target contact → dissipation. Effects follow moving targets and respect direction. Never block the full battlefield view with opaque rectangular artifacts. Author separately; no direct copy from existing games.
7. **Transitions/audio:** ink brush/paper reveal and restrained motion; art remains performant offline at 1280×720 and 390px. Audio only from original/cleared licenses.
