## Era and faction evidence

The Sina race pages describe the Chinese 2009 faction presentation:

- Human: realistic Heroes of Might and Magic III-inspired character art, vivid colors, broadly balanced heroes and troops.
- Elf: ethereal, blurred character styling and pointed ears; high hero attack/speed, fast high-attack units, lower defense, higher cost and slower recruitment.
- Undead: dark/strange styling and muted palette; hero intelligence/attack emphasis; unit defenses/other values generally lower except HP, lower cost and fast recruitment.
- Orc: rough, wild and green styling; high hero attack and unit attack/HP, slower unit speed and recruitment, moderate costs.

These are secondary descriptive statements, not exact combat coefficients. They can guide original replacement art and readability, but do not grant rights to Sina screenshots or Fandom images.

## Data policy

- Keep the current playable English catalog sourced to the dated Wikidot revision. Do not overwrite it with living Fandom values.
- Preserve Chinese names as edition-specific labels. The Chinese source does not prove the international English client used those exact names.
- Treat Fandom names as living community aliases; its page includes later content and changed stats.
- The catalog’s numeric resource costs remain distinct from unit value. The dated Wikidot page states a weighted-resource formula but has three printed Cost/Maintenance discrepancies documented in HG08-EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md. The engine currently computes by the stated formula; original-client capacity behavior for those rows is unverified.
- No gameplay code changed from this crosswalk. It is research evidence, not a claim of complete version parity.
