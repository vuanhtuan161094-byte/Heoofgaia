# Fandom wiki survey — Heroes of Gaia / Castle of Heroes

Survey date: 2026-11-02. This is a source survey for the standalone reconstruction, not a claim that every article or revision was read.

## Coverage and limits

The Fandom landing page reports 608 articles and links to the major subject families: races, buildings, heroes, units, equipment and secondary arms, world objects, action points, rank/fame, spells, PvP and tactics. I reviewed the main mechanics pages and targeted supporting pages that can affect HG-08 through HG-13. Fandom's Special:AllPages and category endpoints were blocked by the available web retrieval interface, so a complete 608-article census and historical revision audit are not available here.

The wiki is community-authored and living. Its own home page labels community content CC BY-SA unless otherwise noted. That statement does not prove the rights for every embedded image, screenshot, sprite, animation, or game file. This project has copied no Fandom art or page text into game assets; this document records short factual summaries with links. Any future asset use still requires a separate provenance and license review.

A Fandom page may combine international and Chinese servers, original launch, Blood Fort, Babel, Rise of the Guilds, and later balance updates. Treat a number as release data only when its page or an independent dated source establishes that build. Keep uncertain values marked as community-reported or edition-unknown.

The available remote article text does not expose trustworthy embedded screenshot files or rights for reuse.

## Implementation decisions

1. Use the wiki as a research index and a secondary community source, not as a single authoritative specification.
2. Prefer dated Chinese and English guides, archived client evidence, or legally accessible original data for exact historical values. Record regional/build differences instead of averaging them.
3. Separate 2009 baseline from Blood Fort, Babel and Rise of the Guilds features. Do not ship tasks, bosses, guild construction projects, castle durability or late equipment as launch-era mechanics without matching release evidence.
4. Use the community battle formula only as a labeled NPC calibration candidate. The published 500-Priest example is one test case, not proof of general PvE or PvP parity.
5. Keep original images, SWFs, sprites and videos as reference-only until ownership or distribution rights are established. Recreate new assets where provenance is unclear.

## Additional scoped review — 2026-11-02

The following Fandom entries were inspected during the continuation survey:

- [Heroes](https://heroesofgaia.fandom.com/wiki/Heroes) gives community equations for hero ATK (unit attack multiplier 1 + ATK/100), DEF (divide damage by 1 + DEF/100), and SPD (add SPD minus 10, multiplied by 0.02, to unit speed; the article claims NPC forces have a hidden SPD-10 hero). Its INT-to-gold text is partially malformed in the rendered page. These are secondary, living-wiki claims and are not independently confirmed against a dated client build.
- The same [Heroes](https://heroesofgaia.fandom.com/wiki/Heroes) page gives Castellan XP as resource-equivalent unit value divided by 3, says to use pre-Manufacturing unit value, and describes combat XP as closer to slain unit value times bonuses divided by 70 while explicitly rejecting division by 40 as incorrect and asking for more testing. Treat both as unverified community models.
- [Hero quality](https://heroesofgaia.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_quality) reports eight candidates per hour at Tavern 10 and a sample table of candidate qualities. The article itself says the rare yellow/green/purple probabilities need more data; its 1-in-10 / 100 / 1,000 values are an early hypothesis, not a confirmed recruitment distribution.
- [Equipment](https://heroesofgaia.fandom.com/wiki/Equipment) lists seven equipment types under its core categories: weapon, helmet, body, combat shinguards, handguards, necklace, and ring. It separately lists eight secondary-arm types. This conflicts with summaries that call the original loadout six slots, so implementation must wait for an actual dated UI/source confirming which types can be equipped together. The page mixes overworld and Bazaar acquisition with Babel floors, later world buildings, and Rise-era/high-level content.
- [Castles](https://heroesofgaia.fandom.com/wiki/Castles) labels durability and siege as “Since Rise Of The Guilds”; the landing page's history announces that expansion in June 2010 and warns that rank statistics became outdated. This is direct evidence that article content spans multiple versions, not a single 2009 specification.

The wiki landing page lists article families for races, equipment, secondary arms, world objects, heroes, and general mechanics, but the Fandom retrieval interface still blocks Special:AllPages, category enumeration, and the MediaWiki all-pages API. This survey remains scoped: it does not represent reading all 608 articles. Outstanding article families include the individual equipment/secondary-arm entries, world-object detail pages, action points, ranks, PvP, cards/items, spells, bugs, tools, story, FAQ, and per-building pages. These remain research tasks; no gameplay values are inferred from their titles alone.

These are useful cross-check leads, but the extracts have no verified historical revision and the values are not applied to the dated catalog.

## Targeted follow-up — Fandom Orc Barracks and dated recruitment update (2026-11-02)

The indexed [Orc Barracks](https://heroesofgaia.fandom.com/wiki/Orc_Barracks) extract exposes its full L1–L10 unlock column: Peon, Wolfrider, Axe Thrower, Direwolf, Ogre, Harpy, Thunderbird, Cyclop, Colossus and Behemoth, with Town Hall L1–L10 respectively. A separate dated Chinese official update notice says the 2010-04-02 maintenance shortened recruitment time for early-, middle- and late-stage units by differing amounts across the races, without publishing per-unit changes. This warns that the 2009 English catalog’s base times are not proven for later Chinese builds. The notice does not resolve Elf, Orc or Undead support gates.

## Dated Tavern quality cross-check — 2026-11-02

The 17173 [2010-03-07 hero-quality guide](https://web.17173.com/content/2010-03-07/1267976868.shtml) reports aggregate hero-stat color bands and states that higher Tavern level is a prerequisite for better heroes and increases their chance. Its White range (≤125) and Blue range (125–160) overlap at 125; the living Fandom [Hero quality](https://heroesofgaia.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_quality) table says 125 or less is White, so the current `nameColor` threshold keeps 125 White. The dated Chinese guide caps ordinary individual stats at 70 and reports Purple totals through 280; Fandom describes 201+ Purple and has no stated maximum in the excerpt. Neither source gives a reliable Tavern rarity distribution. These sources support checking color thresholds, not the deterministic candidate generator or any probability.
