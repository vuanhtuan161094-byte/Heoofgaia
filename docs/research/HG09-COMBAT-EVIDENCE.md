## Limits and next work

- No source found here establishes a complete 2009 international combat formula, morale rule, numeric ranged reach, exact melee movement distance, target-selection algorithm for every command, Stand By defense multiplier, focus damage multiplier, NPC stat table, battle clock, or complete reward/XP rules. The single damage example is calibration evidence, not a complete rule set. SRC29 does not reveal wound probability distribution or treatment queue concurrency.
- The current implementation has no morale system. It must not be reported as original parity.
