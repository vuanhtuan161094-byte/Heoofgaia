## Limits and next work

- No source found here establishes a complete 2009 international combat formula, morale rule, numeric ranged reach, exact melee/retreat movement distance, target-selection algorithm in every edition, NPC stat table, later-patch/international battle timeout, or complete reward rules. The Jan 2009 ZOL report says its build lacks player target selection, while later 2009 guides describe VIP focus; these are kept as an edition conflict. FOCUS currently chooses a target without adding damage. The 10-second auto fallback is reported by the January 2009 Chinese review only. SRC45 supplies only 4★–8★ Chinese-guide XP values; 1★–3★ and non-NPC awards remain modeled. The single damage example is calibration evidence, not a complete rule set. SRC29 does not reveal wound probability distribution or treatment queue concurrency; SRC30's legacy skill note is not original-client verified.
- The current implementation has no morale system. It must not be reported as original parity.
- The included screenshot is captured from this reconstruction running in Chromium. It is not a screenshot of the original game. Original-client side-by-side review remains BLOCKED / NOT TESTED.
