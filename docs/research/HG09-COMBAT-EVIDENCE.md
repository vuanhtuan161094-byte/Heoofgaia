## Limits and next work

- The auto fallback delay is supported by a contemporary review; continuous automatic turns at the local ~900 ms cadence are a UX model only. No reviewed source establishes that cadence.
- Player-selected cell movement and saved multi-turn destinations are reconstruction controls, not evidenced 2009 features. The 12×9 board and Advance-derived SPD ≥7 allowance remain SIMULATED.

- Player-selected cell movement is a reconstruction control enhancement, not an evidenced 2009 feature. Its 12×9 board and reuse of the existing Advance allowance (SPD ≥7 gives melee two cells; ranged one) remain SIMULATED. Do not cite this implementation as proof of historical control or movement parity.

- No source found here establishes a complete 2009 international combat formula, morale rule, numeric ranged reach, exact melee/retreat movement distance, target-selection algorithm in every edition, NPC stat table, later-patch/international battle timeout, or complete reward rules. The Jan 2009 ZOL report says its build lacks player target selection, while later 2009 guides describe VIP focus; these are kept as an edition conflict. FOCUS currently chooses a target without adding damage. The 10-second auto fallback is reported by the January 2009 Chinese review only. SRC45 supplies only 4★–8★ Chinese-guide XP values; 1★–3★ and non-NPC awards remain modeled. The single damage example is calibration evidence, not a complete rule set. SRC29 does not reveal wound probability distribution or treatment queue concurrency; SRC30's legacy skill note is not original-client verified.
- World travel now shares the sourced Hero SPD point increment as a relative-speed proxy, but no recovered source in this register verifies the land-march coefficient. The 20 + 19 seconds per path edge and 40-second floor are local timing models; existing saved trips keep their stored ETA.
- The current implementation has no morale system. It must not be reported as original parity.
- The China profile still has other movement and passive skills unimplemented. Battle Instinct is the one supported movement skill currently implemented. Its 4-round duration/cooldown and fractional-cell carry are explicit local models because SRC57 gives none.
