## Verification boundary

- HG-08 Node/browser tests prove only deterministic behavior in this standalone implementation.
- Latest local regression on 2026-11-02: 76/76 Node, 65/65 installed-Chrome browser checks (14/14 HG-09 browser checks; 13/13 HG-09 Node checks), and 12/12 actual Chromium file:// persistence checks. The exhaustive gate tests exercise every listed unlock requirement positively and negatively and assert that a failed recruit spends no resources or queue slot; Arena recovery tests cover price/time, insufficient funds, v07 save/resume, legacy queue migration, and persistence after a separate browser-process restart. Reincarnation browser checks research/equip the pre-Babel Castellan skill and verify its effect on the actual queue timer. These results do not prove historical parity.
- The enforced Human table follows the later 2009–2010 Chinese edition documented by SRC19/SRC20. Earlier dated SRC27 reports Archer Barracks 2 instead of 3 and does not print the later Town Hall gates; this is recorded as a version conflict, not merged. These Chinese sources do not prove an English client used identical labels or patch timing.
- Vietnamese-language search found a 2010 GameLandVN industry interview confirming the local name “Anh Hùng Online” and Snail Game context, but no Vietnamese numeric troop, Tavern, or combat tables. It is registered for product context only and is not used to invent mechanics.
- Elf/Orc/Undead unit-specific support-building tiers, exact spell-research prices/times, battle XP, Tavern rare-stat odds, physical Android behavior, and original-client screenshot comparison remain **MISSING / BLOCKED**.
- Assets added to this stage must have an owner/license record in `docs/ASSET-PROVENANCE.md`; historical web images are reference-only unless rights are established.

### 2026-11-02 source reconciliation

- The Sina 2009-08-27 race pages provide Chinese unit names and role descriptions for the four ten-unit rosters, but not their numeric per-unit gates. They confirm the scouting role of the tier-2 unit in each race. Preserve the Chinese labels as edition-specific aliases; do not force them onto the international catalog.
- The Sina 2009-08-23 troop-recruitment guide documents the real interaction sequence and says the recruitment screen displays unit details, resource cost, and time. It does not establish the precise support-building or technology gates.
- The Wikidot unit page is explicitly revision 19, last edited 2009-12-06, and remains the dated English catalog used by the current data. Its names/stats differ from the living Fandom table; examples include Orc T1 Imp versus Peon and Green Dragon DEF 199 versus 109. Fandom's current data may describe a later balance/build or an editing error; there is no same-build client observation to settle it.
- The Wikidot page defines unit value as Gold + 5×(Lumber + Ore) + 10×(Sulfur + Crystal), but its printed Cost/Maintenance cells conflict with that formula on at least three rows: Human Heavy Soldier computes to 383 while the cell says 394; Orc Direwolf computes to 428 while the cell says 358; Orc Cyclops computes to 1572 while the cell says 1312. The engine currently derives unit value from resource costs, as the page's prose formula directs. The original client's capacity behavior for these exception rows remains unverified; do not silently change the formula or source values.
- SRC19's Lequ-hosted Human guide and SRC20's 17173 guide have near-verbatim text and are not independent corroboration. The 2010 guide also contains an internal Monk inconsistency: one strategic prose example says Barracks 5 + training technology 5, while its specific unit table and fame-stage recommendation give Town Hall 6 + Barracks 6 + training technology 5. The implementation retains the detailed table row and records this conflict.
