# R04 visual pilot — asset contract

- Asset pack: `r04-original-visual-pilot-v2-action-pose`.
- Purpose: two original character masters integrated into real NL campaign callouts (stage 25 and stage 26), plus one scout ATTACK_CONTACT pose. A real enemy infantry physical action in stage 25 switches the callout image from idle to contact pose for 320 ms, then returns to idle. This is a limited two-pose directional pilot, not a finished roster or animation set.
- Character A: fictional unnamed border scout; young adult male; charcoal lamellar armor, restrained jade accents, short generic spear; front three-quarter facing right.
- Character B: fictional unnamed field tactician; adult female; charcoal armor, burnt-orange sash, small jade command token, short command baton; front three-quarter facing left.
- Neither character depicts a VNG or historical character. Names are temporary role labels.
- Master source: generated RGBA PNGs preserved unmodified as `*-raw-master.png`; registered to a 512×640 RGBA transparent PNG with 56×70 CSS display target and bottom-center pivot. Registration trims transparent margins, scales with Lanczos and composites without changing source alpha. The scout contact pose keeps its generated raw PNG and is registered to 768×640 RGBA with `tools/register_nl_r04_attack.py`, sharing the same bottom-center foot line. Its `--check` mode regenerates pixels and compares RGBA pixels to the hash-locked canonical PNG; PNG compression bytes can vary by platform.
- Visual direction: original Dark Fantasy Sharp-Eyed Chibi, about 3.2 heads tall, painterly material breakup, bright eyes, readable silhouette at small scale.
- Exclusions: no text/UI/frame/logo, copied character, historic insignia/costume, VNG assets or hidden formulas.
- Runtime use: stage 25 bulwark and stage 26 ember battle callouts only. The stage-25 pose switch is gated by an actual campaign event where the enemy infantry unit performs a physical action; it is decorative callout art and does not replace the battlefield unit sprite. Art does not change battle power, unit identity, damage, target, event timing or Save state.
- Provenance route: OpenAI image_gen original generation/editing. Prompts are recorded in `generation_prompts.md`; status `RECREATED`. No source asset from an external game was used.
- Acceptance gates: raw/master hashes, dimensions, alpha bounds and occupancy in JSON; reproducible offline HTML embedding; exact art rendered at desktop 1280×720 and mobile 390×844 with screenshots; Chromium confirms the actual-event switch and idle return. Owner art approval is separate and remains pending. Walk/Hit/Fall frames, other directions, troop identities, alpha VFX and continuous animation are not delivered by this two-pose pilot.
